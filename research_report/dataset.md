# Dataset & Universe Specification

## Universe
Primary Asset Universe:
`AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, NVDA, TSLA, JPM, XOM`

## Modalities
1. **Market Timeseries**: Daily OHLCV bars, Returns, SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR, Volatility.
2. **News Sentiment NLP**: Financial news articles, lexicon sentiment scores, positive/negative probabilities, 1d/3d/5d/7d rolling sentiment.
3. **Fundamental Ratios**: Quarterly Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow metrics, Net Margin, ROE, ROA, Debt/Equity, YoY Growth rates.
