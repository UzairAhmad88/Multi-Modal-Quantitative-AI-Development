# QUANT AI Research Roadmap

## Currently Implemented (v1.0.0)
- Multi-modal dataset synchronization (Market + News + Fundamentals).
- PyTorch `MultiModalQuantNet` learned fusion attention network.
- XGBoost, Random Forest, PyTorch LSTM, GRU & Temporal Transformer models.
- Risk Gate portfolio allocator (25% max position, 40% sector, 1.0x leverage).
- Event-driven backtester with 10 bps fees & 5 bps slippage.
- FastAPI REST backend & Supabase cloud integration.
- Institutional Web OS research terminal & Streamlit dashboard.

---

## Future Roadmap & Expansion
- **Real-Time Streaming**: Websocket streaming ticker bars and live news feeds.
- **Factor Risk Attribution**: Fama-French 5-factor risk decomposition.
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Deep Q-Learning & PPO portfolio execution policy.
- **HuggingFace FinBERT**: Fine-tuned LLM embeddings for earnings call transcripts.
