# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Environment, configuration, logging, testing and repository structure.

## Phase 2 — Market Data
Historical OHLCV ingestion, cleaning, validation and storage.

## Phase 3 — Technical Features
Returns, momentum, moving averages, RSI, MACD, volatility and volume features.

## Phase 4 — News Pipeline
Timestamped news ingestion, normalization and ticker/entity mapping.

## Phase 5 — Financial NLP
Text cleaning, sentiment scoring, embeddings and daily aggregation.

## Phase 6 — Fundamentals
Financial statements, ratios, growth and public-availability alignment.

## Phase 7 — Feature Fusion
Market-only, two-modal and three-modal datasets.

## Phase 8 — ML
Chronological baselines, Random Forest and XGBoost.

## Phase 9 — LSTM / GRU
Sequence generation, scaling, training and checkpointing.

## Phase 10 — Transformer
Small temporal Transformer suitable for local laptop training.

## Phase 11 — Multi-Modal Model
Separate modality encoders and learned fusion.

## Phase 12 — Alpha
Prediction normalization, model ensemble, composite alpha and confidence.

## Phase 13 — Portfolio
Position sizing, allocation, constraints and rebalancing.

## Phase 14 — Risk
Volatility, drawdown, VaR, exposure, leverage and concentration.

## Phase 15 — Backtesting
Time-consistent execution, transaction costs, slippage and performance.

## Phase 16 — Dashboard
Connect completed pipeline outputs to the Streamlit research interface.

## Phase 17 — Testing
Unit, integration and end-to-end testing.

## Phase 18 — Validation
Walk-forward, ablation, sensitivity, regime and error analysis.

## Phase 19 — Finalization
Research report, limitations, reproducibility package and release checklist.
